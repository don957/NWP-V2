Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

Interpreting Visual Texts is most usable for me. My students are getting ready to start a research unit, and I would love to use the graphic organizer for them. Visuals are helpful for struggling readers and challenge-level readers. I think it provides opportunities for good conversations between and among students for interpretation.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
I am not good at establishing routine writing. I do have creative writing every week on my bellwork that students enjoy and I always have intentions to revisit but am not good about following-up to revise.
