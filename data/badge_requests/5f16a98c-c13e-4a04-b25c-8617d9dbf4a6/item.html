How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

It allows students to think more in depth about what they are reading and form more well rounded opinions. They are able to look at different sources of information and heard both sides of an argument. It is also a great way to have the student practice discussions. I think that This idea helps me to more clearly walk students through a break down of how to form well rounded opinions that are back with support.

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
I struggle to do talk and discuss with my student because they get off task easily. I have provided them with sheet of specific questions to ask an answer, but it still feels like if I am not in on the conversation that they are unproductive.
