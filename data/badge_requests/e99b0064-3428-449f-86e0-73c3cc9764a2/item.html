Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

Take a Line for a Walk feels most immediately usable in my teaching context because as an ELA teacher, we are always reading and annotating text and different passages. We go through three different types of writing which are narrative, argumentative, and Expository. I think using the strategy Take a Line for a Walk would work very well with our writing lessons and allow students to choose lines from each of these different types of writings and dive deeper into it. I also think it would be beneficial to add the Take a Line for a Walk strategy to say-mean-matter because they could chose one line from a passage, write about it, and then complete say-mean-matter with it.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
Strategies that I have used to engage my students in routine writing over the course of this school year is doing passes. For example, we write a paragraph of the week. We do five weeks of narrative writing, five weeks of argumentative, and five weeks of expository. If students turn in four in a row on time, they get a pass. This seems to motivate them to get it done.
