Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I am getting better, but TIME is always an issue for me. Even 26 years into my career and 6 years back in the classroom as an ELA teacher, I always seen to push writing aside for more in-depth reading activities. This course has "forced" me to devote more time to writing and I see growth in myself and my students!
