Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

Interpreting Visual Texts was the immediate draw as we need our students to improve their graphing and data analysis skills prior to the EOC in Biology. Also we use a ton of models and I also like to bring in art (STEAM) as well. I also plan to try the Take a Line for a Walk with my students but it was more challenging to implement that this week. Daily Schedule was the hardest for me to connect to our content but I am still giving it some thought. I'm sure I could come up with something!

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
We start every class period with a free write. Getting them to just put pen to paper and brain dump has been a challenge as they freeze up and say "I don't know what to write." I have given a number of prompts and also tell them they can write anything they are thinking about. But many will still answer one of the prompts and then say "I'm done" when I tell them to keep writing. It's a work in progress. We keep at it. I have also switched almost everything we do to paper to cut back on them using google and AI.
