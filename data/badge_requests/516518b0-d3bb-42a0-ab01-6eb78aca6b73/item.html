Name: Cathy Rahder
Badge: Everyday Sparks
Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I have wholeheartedly incorporated the Writer's Notebook. Each student has a composition notebook. I gave all students Post-it notes, and they were given the freedom to organize them the way they saw fit. Some chose 4 post-it notes, some chose 8 post-it notes. The beauty of it was that they were allowed to choose. I also requested UPS to send me free labels for kids to decorate. I received 200 of them. The students get excited when I tell them to pull out their writer's notebooks for multiple uses such as interactive notebook activities, notes, graphic organizers, reflections, or quick writes.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

I will need more composition notebooks now the kids are fully implemented in this process. I have applied for the White River Valley Electric Co. grant asking for more notebooks. I have multiple pics of a student's notebook, but it will only let me upload one pic.
