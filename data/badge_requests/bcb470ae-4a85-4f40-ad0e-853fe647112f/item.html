Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

In my classroom, I have students place all their work in an interactive notebook. I use this as a portfolio of students' work. We write something or do an activity in our notebooks every day, whether it's a quick write or a pre-test question. Sometimes, we even staple or glue our comprehension questions in the notebook. Students use their notebook as a reflection of what they have learned and can use it on their post-test to help formulate a constructive response.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

The challenges that I have found in my classroom is that they struggle writing in cursive or lack the ability to read cursive. They also struggle with writing neatly.
