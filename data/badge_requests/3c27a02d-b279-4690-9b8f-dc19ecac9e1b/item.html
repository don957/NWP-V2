Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

As a social studies teacher, I can myself using the Writing Sprints the most in my context. They can be used to introduce big ideas that will guide student thinking about events. If students begin by reflecting on their own experiences, perhaps it will lead to greater historical empathy. Those connections can make events from the past feel more relevant to teenagers today.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

I have been pleased with the compliance from my students this year. They are willing to make some effort when I include a Writing Sprint in my lessons. One concern I have is the depth of thinking. It's easy to write something down on paper to appease the teacher, but that won't achieve the goals I have for these everyday writing practices.
