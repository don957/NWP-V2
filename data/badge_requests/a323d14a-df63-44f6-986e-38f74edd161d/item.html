Name: Sarah Almon
Badge: Everyday Sparks
Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

As an English teacher, I feel like Quicklists are one of the strategies that are the most usable in my teaching context. They are easy to incorporate at the beginning of any writing unit, and they offer a quick, low stakes method to get students writing down ideas.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

The biggest challenge that I anticipate having will be sticking with the routine in my classroom. It can be difficult to find time to fit in in an already packed class schedule. However, I think I can overcome the issue by restructuring how I do the daily bellringers in my classroom. Turning Monday into a Quicklist day could be a way to solve the problem.
