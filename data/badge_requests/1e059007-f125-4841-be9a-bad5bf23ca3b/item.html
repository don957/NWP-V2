Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

The Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach is so much more organized than ways that I have previously introduced mulitiple texts. I struggle to keep the continued thought process past each day and each hour. Connecting multiple texts like this makes it feel more like one project and continues students deep thinking throughout the week.
