Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I really think Interpreting Visual Text feels most immediately usable in my classroom. Teaching special education, some of my students really struggle with reading and comprehending a text when visual texts are universally understood.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
We usually write to a prompt and mostly do narrative writing. I have recently been letting my students choose their own topic and they have been very creative!
