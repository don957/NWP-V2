How might you introduce Say-Mean-Matter to your students? What about this framework do you think will be helpful for them? What might be challenging?:

I will introduce Say-Mean-Matter to my students by using a slide show. I will begin by giving them a handout of the template to keep in their folder for them to look back on. Then I will use a slideshow to show them one or two student examples from the module to model what it looks like. I find this step important for some of my anxious students so they can see an example before trying something new.

The next slide will have a copy of the template at the top of the slide, followed by the quote. They would set up the template in their writer's notebook. The quote I am picking is the same one I did for this module.

“We are what we repeatedly do. Excellence, then, is not an act but a habit.” — Attributed to Will Durant (often linked to Aristotle)

As for which strategy I am most interested in trying with my students, well, all of them! I don’t know which one I am going to ultimately write about, but I have done a strategy from every module with my biology 2 students.
