Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

The strategy I see using the most from this module is interpreting visual text. Whether it is a picture, graph or motion diagram, they all tell a story. In physics we talk about motion with words, pictures, graphs and equations. Analyzing visual texts perfectly aligns with this multi-modal approach to learning. I regularly use this strategy as bell-ringers or Do Nows. It stimulates deeper thinking, collaboration, and making connections to the content.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
Some of the approaches I have used start with being intentional about incorporating some sort of literacy routine DAILY. Easy ones in my class are science notebooks, quick writes and do nows. I am working up to longer writing with justifications with a CER.
