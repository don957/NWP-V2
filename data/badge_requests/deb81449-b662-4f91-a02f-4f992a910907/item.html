Name: Tomisine R Bazzell
Badge: Joining a Conversation
What is one insight you gained from completing the Atwoodian Table yourself that might shape how you introduce it to students?:

One thing I gained from doing this activity is the knowledge that this is a great way for students to get information in a reliable manner and still be able to form their own opinion on a subject. I will introduce this with fewer people/sources at the table because my students have low reading levels and are still building stamina for reading.

How does the Atwoodian Table idea compare to how you have taught argument and/or responding to multiple texts in the past?:
This is a much better way to look at multiple sources than just taking notes and annotating. It connects the reader to the information and author.
What do you notice about the two sets of excerpts in this module (children in fancy restaurants and toddlers and technology)? How do the excerpts work together to invite students to join the conversation? What are some conversations related to topics you teach?:
I notice that both are about young children. Many of my students have younger siblings or cousins and can relate to both sides of these arguments. Some of the conversation that relates to topics I teach are reading, writing, and children.
