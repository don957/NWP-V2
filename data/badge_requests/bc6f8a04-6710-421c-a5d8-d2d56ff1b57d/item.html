Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I have already started the Daily Schedule strategy with my 8th grade class. They have filled out the clock template. This upcoming week week we are going to use the schedules to do some quick write activities. I'm going to post some questions on the board, and the students are going to pick a question, reflect on their schedule, and write an explanation of their answer based on their schedule. Also, I am going to have them share their schedules with their classmates just to compare how each other's are similar/different.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
We have done a lot of quick writes based on the list we created. They used to use journal prompts from the book, but after making the lists, they choose their own journal prompt from their lists. They are producing more quality writing from this strategy.
