Name: Lindsey Wilson
Badge: Joining a Conversation
What is one insight you gained from completing the Atwoodian Table yourself that might shape how you introduce it to students?:

One insight that I gained from completing the Atwoodian Table that will help me when I introduce it to my students is that there is no right or wrong answer. I feel like students always feel the pressure of not answering something because of the fear of being wrong but knowing that they can write whatever their opinion is and no chance of being wrong I think will give them more confidence to write what they truly think.

How does the Atwoodian Table idea compare to how you have taught argument and/or responding to multiple texts in the past?:
I have always taught with just two different pieces and I think having the extra pieces and having the students summarize in their own words will make them understand all sides of the argument.
What do you notice about the two sets of excerpts in this module (children in fancy restaurants and toddlers and technology)? How do the excerpts work together to invite students to join the conversation? What are some conversations related to topics you teach?:
I noticed that each of the two sets had at least one view of support or being against and I think this will invite the students to be more open about their honest opinion. When we start our argumentative unit we one of the topics we use for conversation is should smart phones be allowed in schools.
