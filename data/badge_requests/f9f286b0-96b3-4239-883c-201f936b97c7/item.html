What is one insight you gained from completing the Atwoodian Table yourself that might shape how you introduce it to students?:

One insight I gained from completing the Atwoodian Table myself that might shape how I introduce it to students is modeling it for the students. At first, when I saw it, I thought that it it was going to be more complicated than it turned out being. After completing it, I liked how it put all the information from each author of a text on one paper. I think the students will be able to grasp it better if we do one first as a class and see the outcome.

How does the Atwoodian Table idea compare to how you have taught argument and/or responding to multiple texts in the past?:
The Atwoodian Table idea compares to how I have taught argument in the past by having all the information accessible and easy to read and cite.

The thing I noticed about the sets of excerpts in this module are differing opinions. They work together because the students can see opposing side easily. A conversation I will use is the argument of AI being a threat to their generation.
