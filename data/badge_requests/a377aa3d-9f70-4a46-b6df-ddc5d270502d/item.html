Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

I learned that my students obtained a deeper understanding because the whole process of reading, writing and discussion are connected. My students understood better during discussion , instead of writing. They also understood and expressed ideas more clearly during the writing portion. Speaking to a group still causes some of my students to be hesitant, and learning there are varied ways to learn helps them overcome some of this hesitance.
