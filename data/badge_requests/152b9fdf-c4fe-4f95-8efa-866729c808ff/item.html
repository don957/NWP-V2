Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

All of these strategies just really encouraged me to slow down and approach our work more thoughtfully. Now I did this in an elective class where we have more time and it was wonderful to slow down and be more intentional with our thinking and our learning. Some of the strategies will also be useful even in a core class that has much swifter pacing. Go slow to go fast? Is that the expression? Sometimes slowing down and doing the thing with more intention and connection will help the students learn and develop the skills faster than the break neck pace at which we are usually forced to learn.
