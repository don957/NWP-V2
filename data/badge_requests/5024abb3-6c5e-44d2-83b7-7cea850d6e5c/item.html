Name: Stacey Kay
Badge: Everyday Sparks 2.0
Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

Take a Line for a Walk is the most usable for me because it applies to my everyday context. It is a strategy I can use anytime since we always have text available.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
Oh, my! There are so many! I am blessed to work with dual credit students most of the day, plus I also have media students. My students are excited to see their work published. Sometimes my students are not engaged if they know that it will not be included as part of a published piece in our media program or if it is not for a grade in my dual credit classes.
