Name: Haley Murray
Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

The Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach helps students dig deeper into multiple texts. I normally teach claim as the first thing we do, but after reading this module, I don't think that is the best approach. This layered strategy feels more natural. It gives students the ability to access information first which I think will make forming their own claims easier. I think using this method could shift how I guide students by giving them more structure to explore ideas before they have to make a final claim.

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
I alway encourage conversation. Every time we write papers, students get to explain their ideas to a partner and walk through their thinking process. It is a great way to verbally think through ideas. My biggest challenge is that students get off task and have to be redirected. I try to give structured time so it does not happen often.
