Name: Shelby Vinson
Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

This strategy relates to how I have previously taught students to mark up an article or text to find the important details. This process encouraged me to do more with their annotations, like digging deeper and creating a concept map to organize their thoughts or opinions.

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
Right now my students engage in discussion to compare answers and bounce ideas off of one another. I think having activities like this encourage conversations that are deeper and lead to more ideas being shared. I have encountered my students not being very willing to talk to classmates that they are not friends with, and they struggle to have sustained focused conversations that are on task.
