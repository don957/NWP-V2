Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

Interpreting Visual Texts. We have already had success with this and it feels like an easy addition to our daily routine of quick writes in our notebooks. Plus, as I mentioned in discussion, I'm already thinking about using images in the early part of our next unit on research and inquiry, so this fits really nicely with that. We can start looking at images and thinking about them and then I can do the gallery walk to start sparking investigation for our research project. I haven't planned the unit out yet, so these resources are incredible!

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
We have continued using the They Say/I Say and Say-Mean-Matter protocols on our articles of the week (I think we are in week 9 of these). As well as continued quick writes. One challenge is always just how much we have to do and the interruptions that just come along during the school year. I'd love to do more writing every day, but I am also trying to prioritize vocab and test prep, etc. But we do our best!
