Name: Baily Stevenson
Badge: Teacher as Researcher: Cultivating Literacy Routines
Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

I noticed that there are other ways to deep read and annotate other than just marking up the text with literary devices and summarizing. I like the idea of switching up the strategies so that students don’t get bored of the same strategies. It made me realize that my teaching has become repetitive by using the same strategies I’m comfortable with. Engaging in this process has given me a refresher for my teaching. It has encouraged me to look at work that I have done over and over and revise it to be more engaging and helpful.

Share one artifact or anecdote from trying out your chosen strategy (e.g., a piece of student work, notes from your Writer’s Notebook, a classroom snapshot, or a brief story from the experience).:
What new questions or next steps are you leaving this module with? How might you continue to explore or build on this inquiry in your future teaching?:

My next steps include trying each of these strategies we learned about with all of my classes to see how different students work with different strategies. I want to apply it to various unit topics to see which it fits the best with. My goal is to continue revising one assignment each quarter to be more engaging and include a new activity.
