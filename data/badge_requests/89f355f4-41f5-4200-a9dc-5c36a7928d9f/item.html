Name: Donna Bone
Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

I have found that using this approach has made my students more willing to participate in discussions. They have time to organize thoughts in writing , and also gain knowledge from different perspectives. I feel my learning environment is becoming more effective and students are gaining more reading comprehension, communication skills and critical thinking skills.

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
Students at times are hesitant to participate. Gaps in skills such as communication and vocabulary also can hinder participation. The strong students also take over. Opportunities can include setting expectations ahead of times, assign roles. Checking in with students one on one also helps.
