Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I feel like incorporating more visual texts is what is immediately usable for me in science. This module reminded me that students are used to visuals which got me thinking about how visuals are in their EOC's and other standardized testing (sigh). Being able to make sense of visuals is a transferrable skill that will serve them in the future, regardless of the path they choose. I also think I can have some fun with take a line for a walk in my forensics class.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
I have added more response writing to my everyday work. Students often think one word answers a question, when it does not. Being consistent with my expectations/non negotiables is helping. For example, having them write in complete sentences, capital letter, punctuation, restate in your own words. However, some still insist on googling the answer and pasting it in. Not even pasting it as plain text LOL. Now, I return the assignment for them to redo using the text that was provided (which is stated in the directions and verbally....several times).
