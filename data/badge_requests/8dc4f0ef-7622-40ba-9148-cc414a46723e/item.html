Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

The Writer's Notebook feels the most immediately usable in my teaching context. With a Writer's Notebook, students are allowed to keep all of their ideas and work in one spot. Eventually, they could start keeping their quicklists and writing sprints all in one spot as well. Writer's Notebooks is a house for all of the writing that will take place throughtout the year.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

I think the biggest challenge is going to be for students to keep it organized. They will have their tabs that hold each section, however just ensuring they will remember to go where they need to.
