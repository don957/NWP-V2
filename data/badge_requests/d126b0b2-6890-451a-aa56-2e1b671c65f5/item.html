Name: shelly strickfaden
Badge: Joining a Conversation
What is one insight you gained from completing the Atwoodian Table yourself that might shape how you introduce it to students?:

I really liked that is was a table. I was able to put myself and "others" at the table easily because I am already familiar with sitting at a table and talking to others. I also liked that it was a round table so there are no sides. If it was not round I could see people getting wrapped up in where to properly "seat" people. I think this is a good way to introduce synthesizing viewpoints because it is familiar. I also liked the example using post it notes for the participant blurbs. My students love post-it so I think that would help in their willingness to do this. I also really like that excerpts were used. The readings were short and non-overwhelming. i think that will help students gain comfort with summarizing and organizing thoughts.

How does the Atwoodian Table idea compare to how you have taught argument and/or responding to multiple texts in the past?:
It has used more perspectives that I have done. I have mostly focused on CER writing and using evidence to prove/disprove a claim. I liked that this had multiple perspectives from different sides of the issue.
What do you notice about the two sets of excerpts in this module (children in fancy restaurants and toddlers and technology)? How do the excerpts work together to invite students to join the conversation? What are some conversations related to topics you teach?:
They are non-threatening issues. They are not content specific and thus are more free to allow opinion formation. They is no presumed right content answer. I also think your perspective can change depending on where you are in life- are you a kid, parent, etc. I like that the excerpts provided opposing perspectives and some more moderate middle thoughts. They gave a range of views from expert to parent. I thought it was good coverage. The work to invite students in because many students are already familiar with these topics (at least the technology topic). Conversations related to my subject- global warming and climate change and various other earth science issues.
