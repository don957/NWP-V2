Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

I realized that I need to spend a lot more time modeling the process. It was easy for me to forget the struggles that I had in thinking deeply the first time that I did the Say-Mean-Matter activity, and I do not feel like I broke it down and modeled the process well enough. Without having tried it myself first, I don't know that I would have been able to pinpoint the problems as easily, and I would not have been able to self-reflect on my teaching as well.
