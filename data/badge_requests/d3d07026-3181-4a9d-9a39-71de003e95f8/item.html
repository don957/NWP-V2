Badge: Teacher as Researcher: Cultivating Literacy Routines
Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

I noticed about my students that at first making a quick list was a struggle for them. It was hard for them to take things at a slower pace. Once they worked on it for awhile they realized that it wasn't that big a deal. When we got to the point for them to write about one item from their list, it took the pressure off of them to think about what they wanted to write. Students were then eager to write and could focused more of their attention on including details in their writing. I found that students wanted to spend more time writing when they were able to make a quick list before a longer piece of writing. This process made me shift my thinking that I should incorporate low stakes writing more often. This allows students to think freely and are more encouraged to write when the pressure is off.
