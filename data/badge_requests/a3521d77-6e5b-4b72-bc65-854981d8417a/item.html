Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I found the interpreting visual text the easiest to implement first. We talked about maps in the social studies classes I support. It was interesting to see how they interpreted the different visuals that I showed. Even though they were of the world, they showed different information.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
I have used a stop and everybody writes routine within my ELA class. I used questions I would generally ask to lead up to a higher order thinking task, but instead of having them answer verbally, I have them write so I can see everyone's thinking before we talk about it.
