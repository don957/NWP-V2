Name: Anna Cummins
Badge: Joining a Conversation
What is one insight you gained from completing the Atwoodian Table yourself that might shape how you introduce it to students?:

I think students having to make a concise claim that isn't explicit will be good but challenging for students. I don't do this enough. I often aske them to highlight the claim in the text or make their own based off their opinions, but to make one in your own words about someone's else stance is challenging.

How does the Atwoodian Table idea compare to how you have taught argument and/or responding to multiple texts in the past?:
I normally use just 2 texts, one on each side of an argument, to teach argument. However, I am seeing with Layered Writing and this strategy that more sources/arugments the better.
What do you notice about the two sets of excerpts in this module (children in fancy restaurants and toddlers and technology)? How do the excerpts work together to invite students to join the conversation? What are some conversations related to topics you teach?:
Both excerpts draw kids in because they have had their own personal experiences. These topics are issues that they know something about. We just finished reading WWII lit circle books and some of the topics that have come up relate to our political climate today and students were able to identify with some of the voices they are hearing today.
