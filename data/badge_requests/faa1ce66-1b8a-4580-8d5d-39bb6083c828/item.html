Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

As a middle school teacher, I’ve found that students are often more willing to engage with a visual before diving into complex informational text. I often pull visuals before reading to assess what students already know about the topic before we read as well as using them as a hook to draw them into the topic. Using visuals also supports all learners. Visuals provide an accessible starting point for struggling readers, English learners, and students who may feel intimidated by traditional texts. At the same time, they offer depth for advanced students who can analyze nuance, bias, and rhetorical choices.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
Other strategies or approaches I have used are collaborative writing tasks, writer's notebooks, and sentence stems. One challenge I have faced is writing stamina. Students lose engagement with writing task quickly or finish quickly without putting too much thought into their writing.
