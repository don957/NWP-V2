Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

I learned that some of my students aren't performing to their best abilities a lot of the time; I was quite surprised at the work and focus I got out of some of them. I feel I need to up my teaching game a bit to give them a chance to reach their full potential.

I got quite a bit of push back when it came time for the students to write. I was surprised by one student who complained about the process, then her writing was very passive-aggressive ("we had to watch a video," "we had to read an article," etc.) but then her final paragraph changed tone and said she was glad we did this activity...it just reinforces that we should continue to push our students; they will be grateful for it at some point.
