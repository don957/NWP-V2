Name: Glenita Montgomery
Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

As a math teacher, I haven't really used these approaches in the past. I see now how important it is to give students multiple opportunities to experience this type of argumentative writing. I would have loved participating in writings like this when I was in school. I will continue to look for ways to add writing into my instruction now.

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
I use Number Talks and Problem of the Day to get students warmed up and thinking about math each day. They solve on their own and then we discuss multiple ways that people solved the same problem. I encourage students to talk to each other when they are working in groups at the wipebooks around the room. If they ask me a question, I ask what their partner/s thought and what they have tried so far. I like to ask, "Can you tell me more about your thinking?" My biggest challenge is definitely time constraints. I am really behind on math standards and the testing window is getting closer every day:(
