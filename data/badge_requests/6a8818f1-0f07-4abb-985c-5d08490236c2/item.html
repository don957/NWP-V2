Name: Shannon Tuley
Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

I have used layered writing in the past, as it is encouraged with our text book. The difference is the texts included literature as well. This will definitely shift how I teach argument writing and how to form their own claims and I am very excited to slowly build on what they already know with multiple perspectives to help them with making an informed claim - rather than asking them to make the claim and then collect the evidence.

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
I am a very big think-pair-share user. With almost every question we answer in class, I ask that they think, then share with a partner first. I do this because sometimes students get asked a question, and they aren't sure exactly how to answer or they are scared to give a wrong answer. This gives them an opportunity to learn from someone else and bounce ideas off of each other first. I have had challenges with the quiet students still not speaking as much, and my more talkative students getting off topic, so I have learned to set a timer and keep it short!
