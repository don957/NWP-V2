Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

I think the teacher- researcher process really allows us to focus on implementation and reflection. I think we can get caught up in the day- to- day hustle and bustle that we forget to truly reflect on our activities and examine them for ways we can improve for next year. As this module progressed, I was able to allow myself to dedicate intentional time to planning, implementation, and reflection of a singular activity.

What new questions or next steps are you leaving this module with? How might you continue to explore or build on this inquiry in your future teaching?:

The main thing I learned from this module is that even implementing one new activity/ lesson is a win! I get very overwhelmed at the thought of every lesson being perfect, or every lesson including every activity or learning experience available. This sometimes causes me to spin out and end up not trying anything new! With this framework of intentional implementation, I have equipped myself with the tools necessary to feel empowered to try new things!
