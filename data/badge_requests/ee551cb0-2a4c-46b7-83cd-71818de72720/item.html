How might you introduce Say-Mean-Matter to your students? What about this framework do you think will be helpful for them? What might be challenging?:

In an introduction of the Say-Mean-Matter to my students I would ask them about things that are important to them. How they matter and the impact that they have on them. This would help to create a foundation for their thinking in giving them something simple and tangible to relate to, before throwing and abstract idea such as a quote at them. A framework that is always very beneficial to my students is to use a graphic organizer. This allows them to see how their think and processing should go together, while also making sure that all the part of their think is there.
