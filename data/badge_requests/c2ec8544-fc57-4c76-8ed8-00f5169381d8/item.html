Name: Trisha Voellinger
Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

There are definitely more steps in the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach than what I have previously done in the past. I have always started with state the claim and then find research to back your claim. However, after reading through the example of homework, I am going to try it this week with the same topic and follow the steps as written. I am curious to see how this process will guide my students toward forming their own claims.

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
There is little talk and discussion in my classroom right now. We do think-pair-shares occasionally, but not as often as I would like. It is challenging because I have 50 minute periods with them, and it seems like we run out of time. It is also a challenge with a few classes to keep them focused and not get off on sidebar conversations.
