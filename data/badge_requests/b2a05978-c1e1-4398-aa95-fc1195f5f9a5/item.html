Badge: Teacher as Researcher: Cultivating Literacy Routines
Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

Looking back on this inquiry cycle, I learned that I need to place more emphasis on modeling the use of imagery in students’ writing. Although this was a quick write, I believe that even incorporating slight imagery can support students as they begin to expand on their initial lists. Typically, I only glance at these quick lists since they are intended primarily for students’ own use, but this task gave me the opportunity to take a closer look at this early stage of their writing process. Moving forward, I plan to model imagery more intentionally during instruction, which will help students develop a routine of using descriptive language naturally, often without even realizing they are doing it.
