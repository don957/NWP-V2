Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I really like both the Take a Line for a Walk and Interpreting Visual Texts. I use the NYT Picture of the Week as a routine already in my classroom and my students are always excited to find out the Big Reveal on Fridays. I'm exciting to try Take a Line for a Walk because the fact that it is one line feels approachable to students AND it is another way for them to enter a conversation with the text, like in Layered Writing.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
I tried the Layered Writing and am excited to share my writing samples. The biggest challenge I'm running into is handing over some of this freedom to students. I work in a district with a significant population of EL students so all students are constantly fed sentence starters. I want to move away from sentence starters (still use an accommodation, not a universal for students) so that students can better develop their own writing voice.
