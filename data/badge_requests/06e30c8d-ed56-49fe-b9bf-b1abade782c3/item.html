Badge: Joining a Conversation
What is one insight you gained from completing the Atwoodian Table yourself that might shape how you introduce it to students?:

I noticed throughout this that the background of the person sharing the information shaped what they said and how they talked about the topic. For example, AAP discussed specifically the health risks involved, since that is what they are primarily focused on when it comes to children. I would want the students to keep this in mind when they are reading any argument, so I would make sure to introduce it with the texts.

How does the Atwoodian Table idea compare to how you have taught argument and/or responding to multiple texts in the past?:
I think this is a much better way to teach argument and responding to texts. Usually I just have them take notes, and then write a comparison. But the visual of this would be a better way to get it.
What do you notice about the two sets of excerpts in this module (children in fancy restaurants and toddlers and technology)? How do the excerpts work together to invite students to join the conversation? What are some conversations related to topics you teach?:
Both sets of excerpts have to do with topics that would be relevant to the students in the classroom. A line could also be drawn between them to do a compare/contrast. Because it is something that directly impacts them, they can feel like they have more of a say in the conversation.
