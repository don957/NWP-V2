Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

Take a line for a walk and interpreting visual texts are the most immediately useable in my teaching context. I really like the take a line for a walk for more everyday-type of writing and the interpreting visual texts for argument writing, which I plan to do very soon. The take a line for a walk is great because students can pick lines that stick out to them, or are meaningful/relevant to them, and make connections/dive deeper in what the text is saying.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
I have used a layered writing technique several times this year. We get an essential question with each unit in our curriculum. Students write an answer to the question before reading any of the texts. Each text explores something related to the question, and after each reading, students add on to (whether they continue on the same path or they have changed their mind) their original answer. They must use examples or evidence from the text in their answers. The challenges I have encountered with this and any sustained writing is that, even with a rubric, many times I get bare minimum answers. Some students are okay with bare minimum work, and some are not but their idea of full potential work is different than mine.
