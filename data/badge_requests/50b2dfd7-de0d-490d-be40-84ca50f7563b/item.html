Name: Trisha Boyles

Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I actually use Writer's Notebooks, but I certainly feel that both the Quicklists and Writing Sprints can be implemented immediately in my practice. I could even tweak these lists to reflect specific connections I might want students to get from pieces of literature or other texts. This could be an excellent companion to close reading and academic response tasks.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

Honestly, I think the biggest challenge with adolescent writers is getting them to be brutally open and honest. While a few will really open up, I am finding students are worried about what the audience will think of their thinking and experiences, and, of course, their writing. I often try to remind them that writing can be a great way to get our thoughts out and reflect on them with a more objective eye; I also try to remind them that their thoughts are safe with me (unless they are being harmed in some way).
