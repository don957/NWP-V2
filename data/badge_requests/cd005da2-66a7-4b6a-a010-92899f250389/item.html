Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

The Quicklists strategy feels most immediately usable within my teaching context. I work with students who struggle to write simple sentences, and quicklists seem like they will reduce some of the stressors that occur when I ask my students to write.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

I anticipate the biggest challenge to be "buy-in" with bringing daily writing into my classroom. My students are embarrassed by their struggles, and many of them struggle with writing and spelling. I will allow students to use speech-to-text, type, or write to create their lists. I will also permit students to announce words that I will write quickly on the board in a "word bank" to help with spelling. For students who receive scribing, I will write what they tell me, and then they may either type or write while copying my writing.
