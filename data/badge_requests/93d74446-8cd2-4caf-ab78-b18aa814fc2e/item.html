What is one insight you gained from completing the Atwoodian Table yourself that might shape how you introduce it to students?:

An insight I've gained is providing a structure to how students are using text evidence in their own arguments. When researching, my students are simply information mining--they just want to find evidence to support their own claims. This reframes argument and research as a conversation. I used this process in Layered Writing but I'm excited to use it throughout the entire school year next year.

I think both sets show a diverse range of opinions which allows students to make their own connections to the piece, which we know increases engagement. I'm actually working on building text sets about disabilities, genocides, and poverty which are topics in my 7th grade ELA class.
