Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

Looking back, I learned that I am very good at scaffolding and modeling for my students, but this is more about them and them connecting to reading. I love how this layering strategy gives the students ownership and connections that I can't make for them. I have decided that it is so good for students to take ownership of their writing that I will be doing this more often. I still need to build writing stamina for the students and will start of the year with a writing journal next year.
