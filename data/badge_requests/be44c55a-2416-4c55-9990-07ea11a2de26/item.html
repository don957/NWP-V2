Name: Trisha Boyles
Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

I have done a lot of giving students directions or specific questions to respond to as we read texts in hopes of helping them engage. This approach actually puts more onto the student and allows them to have more control over how they engage with text. I really appreciate this part and am glad to have this slight refinement in my teaching!

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
I actually work in a great deal of partner discussion. I feel that reading, writing, and talking go hand-in-hand when we really want students to process thinking. One of the challenges is keeping students on topic and reigning them back in after talk time is up!
