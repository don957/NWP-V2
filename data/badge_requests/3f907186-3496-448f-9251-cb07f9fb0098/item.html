Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

I feel like using this process helped me to discover the exact things that make some of students hesitant with writing. Many of them got stuck at different parts, and I was able to see a correlation to their struggles in my other classroom activities that include writing. The breaking down of pieces that lead up to the final writing really helped me pinpoint where students needed guidance.
