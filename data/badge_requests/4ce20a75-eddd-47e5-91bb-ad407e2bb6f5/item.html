Name: Torey Stevens
Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

It helps the students to perform more structured research on a topic before they try to submit their own writing on a researched argument. Before students tend to just look for research that supports an opinion they already hold rather than researching a topic and then forming an opinion. The process will have students form more informed claims and also allow for stronger counter arguments (at least in my opinion).

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
The talk is mostly me asking questions and the students answering them (if you wait long enough). Through this technique they get the chance to discuss the sources with each other and how the author has come to their conclusion. It also gives students a chance to "see" other's opinion and hear how they support it. It gives them more practice defending their claims/opinions as well. The challenge has been for some of the "weaker" students to not just mimic what other's in their group have already said.
