Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I'm currently using interactive notebooks in my classroom. However, I notice that I haven't been engaging the students in the writing prompt sections of their notebooks as much as I would like. I feel like the writing quicklists and sprints will be most immediately usable. I feel like these would be useful; because one, I already have notebooks set up for them, and two, the writing sprints will be an "quick" way to easing my students (and myself) into writing more during our class time together.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

I think my own time management will be a challenge and opposition from the students on writing even more rather than them getting to type all of their answers. I will have to be strict with my self in creating the time in class for them to write, or create more homework opportunities for my students to write in their notebooks.
