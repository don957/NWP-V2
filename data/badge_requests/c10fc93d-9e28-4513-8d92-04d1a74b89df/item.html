Name: Shaylee Rademacher
Badge: Everyday Sparks
Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

My students have a Science notebook, and I would like to use it as a Writer's Notebook. I would like to use quicklists, and writing sprints in the Science/writer's notebook. The reason that I can immediately use it is because they already have the notebook, so it wouldn't be one more thing they have to keep track of, which is quite the challenge for middle schoolers.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

The biggest challenge will be time. Teaching my science curriculum takes every minute of every day. I try not to waste time and fill the hour as much as possible. To address it, I want to use writing as a way for students to better learn the standards. I am going to make a goal of writing in our notebooks (just for writing, not notes, or models, etc) once a week. That seems doable right now.
