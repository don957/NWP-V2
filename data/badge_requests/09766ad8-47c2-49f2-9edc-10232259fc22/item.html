Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

I learned that my students are much better writers when there is less pressure on the structure of the writing and they can write about what they are interested in. I don't typically assign writing that doesn't grade correct grammar, spelling, and punctuation, so giving an assignment like that helped my to notice what abstract thought they were capable of. It made me see the value in free form writing.
