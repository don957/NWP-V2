Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I think the visual text task is the most usable in my teaching right now because, like the text said, my students are very driven by visuals. They fill their free time with videos, gifs, memes, and random images on social media. I think adding visual aids to complement (or oppose) texts we read would be an effective way to increase their understanding of concepts they would normally be too complex (or too boring!). I think this is an excellent strategy, and I can't wait to incorporate it in my English 4 class that is reading All The Light We Cannot See.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
We LOVE Say, Mean, Matter. We use it for bellringers, to review before reading, to analyze complex texts, when reading articles, and so much more. We love that it can be quick and effective, or we can use it to dig deeper and really get to the heart of a text. As for challenges I've encountered, I actually discovered that my students love to write! I expected pushback, but they have expressed almost all positive feedback. They do sometimes complain about struggling to come up with ideas, but this program has given me plenty of ideas to help them. Overall, this has been by FAR the most beneficial training I've ever had.
