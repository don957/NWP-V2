Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

Students as well as myself, was more willing to try this method because I knew what would be expected of me. I think my students could say the same. Overall I was really hesitant because I lack confidence in my writing abilities. I had a total of 14 students with only 3 of those students being 8th graders while the rest were 7th graders. I think the 8th graders had some confidence in their writing due to being a grade higher and more experience, however I think this benefited my 7th graders to see and how much effort they were willing to put forth towards each task asked of them. I LOVED the layering piece, because I think it allowed students to build from where they were and then gain some confidence. Since everyone had the same resources I also think that did level the playing field and students knew they had good information as opposed to worrying about is my resource reliable.
