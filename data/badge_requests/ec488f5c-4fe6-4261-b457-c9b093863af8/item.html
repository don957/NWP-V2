Name: Shannon Bicksler
Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

I think this is a good way to practice looking at multiple sets of evidence for CER. I think I would use it for CER rather than writing an argumentative type paper at the end. I think before I would usually have them read the texts together and make a claim but I like that this process breaks it down into smaller pieces so they have opportunities to slow down and engage with the texts.

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
I've always struggled with having students conversate with each other. I have tried to start implementing things like "Think, pair, share" or just talking among their group. I think the challenge I have the most is making these conversations meaningful. They tend to want to get the conversation over with as fast as possible and don't really engage with each other and the topic. Sometimes if they are really interested then they will. They also really enjoy the arguments I have found because they can defend their claim against other students.
