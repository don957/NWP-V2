Name: Carissa Schroer
Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

It is really similar to what I've used before. This provides organization for capturing thinking that I did not previously have. I think that I constantly feel rushed. This might require me to slow down some and I'm not sure how that will work. I will have to refine it while using it, which is so often the case!

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
We constantly use turn and talks and discussions. We have planned pop up debates. We also talk in small groups - in their teams and with me. I find that sometimes they get off track, or the talk doesn't make it to the paper. I think these layers will help with that.
