Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

Using a version of the Atwoodian table while doing this - to help them keep track the info in the texts, they really got into this. I was worried they would think it silly. I think we all go with the one and done theory and this proves again that repetition and adding layers is helpful. Unfortunately, I found myself hurrying and should not be. Organization is still an issue-helping them keep these ready for the next day...not having to start over because they lost their papers. I am now keeping this project in the classroom to prevent that.
