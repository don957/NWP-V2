Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

I learned the importance of previewing an activty, especially if it's one I haven't led before. Students will mirror my hype, or rush through the things that I seem to rush through. I will especially keep this in mind when previewing novels that my students read in class. The reading experience is much more fun when students look forward to the book we're reading.
