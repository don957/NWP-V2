Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

Looking back I learned that some of my students who don't usually want to participate in things opened up a bit more with the different strategies that were used. Engaging in this process has helped shift my thinking on introducing multiple strategies over the course of a few days, because I used to think this would confuse them more then help them.

A moment that stands out is when I had a student ask "is there really no wrong answer or are you just trying to trick us into participating?" This was meaningful because it showed me that how worried some of these students are to participate knowing there is a chance they can be wrong and how worried they are about what other think of them.
