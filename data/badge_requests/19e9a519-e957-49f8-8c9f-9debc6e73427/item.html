Badge: Everyday Sparks
Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

Since we have little to no time to add things I plan to split it into a 4 day process. We are a 4 day week district. Day 1 they write the 11 ideas. That's it. Day 2 - reflect on each again and pick 2 and tell me in 30 seconds why you picked those 2. Day 3 - do the 3 minute write. Day 4 - share and discuss what they thought.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

Fitting it into the time frame we have without pushing off something we HAVE to get done. This could be in lieu of their regular Bellwork.
