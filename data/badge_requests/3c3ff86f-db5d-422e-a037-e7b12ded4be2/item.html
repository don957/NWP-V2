Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

The writer's notebooks seem that they would be the easiest and quickest thing that I could immediately use in my classroom. I see them as an opportunity to allow students more time for writing that doesn't necessarily have to be formal.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

I think that the biggest challenge is, "I can't". I hear this daily. I always follow up with, "Maybe you don't think you can, but let's try." Students write themselves off many times without giving things a try. I encourage everyone to try no matter what. I tell them that they will never learn if they don't try or practice. I provide individual feedback which helps immensely.
