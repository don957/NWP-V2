How might you introduce Say-Mean-Matter to your students? What about this framework do you think will be helpful for them? What might be challenging?:

I have already introduced the Say-Means-Matters strategy to my students, and I loved it. We were reading the play adaptation of The Diary of Anne Frank, so I was very excited to model this strategy with my favorite quotes. I simply broke down the strategy with my students and modeled a quote from the play with them. I knew the hardest part of this strategy, for my students, would be the “Say” part. They struggle so much with paraphrasing and summarizing. And sure enough, after they worked this strategy with their own quotes, “Say” was the struggle. I will continue to work with them on paraphrasing and summarizing, but I was very impressed with my students on the “Means/Matters” part of this strategy. I knew this strategy would help with deeper comprehension/thinking, and it did. I loved hearing conversations throughout this lesson, and I found myself thinking, “Wow, they are getting it!”
