What is one insight you gained from completing the Atwoodian Table yourself that might shape how you introduce it to students?:

Like I said in the discussion, I liked this strategy. I feel like it's bordering on a graphic organizer without really going to far that direction; it also feels like it could be treated or turned into more of a sketchnote-type activity. I think it would be great to start it with some very low-stakes arguments so they can get comfortable with it before really digging into scientific claims. I also think it might be fun to let the kids take turns sitting around a table, making arguments of their own about a given topic, while the others take notes in this format and draw their own conclusions based on what their classmates have presented.
