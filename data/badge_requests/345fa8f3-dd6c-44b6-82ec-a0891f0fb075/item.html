How might you introduce Say-Mean-Matter to your students? What about this framework do you think will be helpful for them? What might be challenging?:

I teach 6th grade ELA in a middle school, and in order to keep students with me throughout a lesson - especially during the later hours of a day- I am always looking for ways to use Tik Tok or Youtube videos. I would probably find an entertaining-type funny animal video or funny science-experiment-gone-wrong video -safe for school- in order to introduce the SAY-MEAN-MATTER strategy. I think that using a video like that would make a great anticipatory set for middle schoolers. I loved that the SAY-MEAN-MATTER graphic organizer that we were given to copy was all about argumentative writing, which is something we have just started to work on. I created a Google slideshow to show to students in order to help teach the strategy and to help them get started with argumentative writing. I haven't yet had a chance to spruce it up, but it would look something similar to what I have uploaded below. I would probably share with the students the fact that my granddaughter, a kindergartener- and I were using this strategy together when collecting leaves one day. The kids love to hear the stories associated with my own kids and grandkids.
