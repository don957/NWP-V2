Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I used interpreting visual texts. I made an anchor chart on Canva. I co-taught this strategy with our math teacher. We picked visual text that went along with math since we were in her content class. As a title teacher, I either pull small groups or push into content area classrooms to provide support. I would definitely use this strategy in my small reading groups because I work with struggling readers, and visual texts require less reading. I love that this strategy is low stakes.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
I have used quick writes through out the school year. I chose them because my small reading groups are 20-20 minutes long, so I can implement quick writes and still cover skills for my reading interventions. I have noticed over the year that students are applying phonics and morpheme skills in their writing. A challenge that I still face is some students are still reluctant to write. A challenge overall this year is fitting in writing routines, Aspire Bridge to Practice, and cover skills that students have a deficit in. I have noticed through all the trainings the importance of reading/writing connection.
