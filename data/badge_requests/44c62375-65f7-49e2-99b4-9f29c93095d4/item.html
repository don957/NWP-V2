Badge: Teacher as Researcher: Cultivating Literacy Routines
Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

Since we did say, mean, matter as group discussions, I found that my students did actually have a pretty good grasp of analysis when they could talk it out instead of writing. Even some of my students who struggle the most with analysis excelled really well when they had to orally provide that analysis, and my students who are often more successful with analysis pushed the analysis of their peers further by asking questions. It reminded me that it might be better to start the year with some say, mean, matter strategies where students talk them out in their pods before they trying writing their analysis out. It would also give me an opportunity to praise really good analytical statements so that students had more of an opportunity to hear what good analysis looks like before they have to try it on their own in a more isolated process.

What new questions or next steps are you leaving this module with? How might you continue to explore or build on this inquiry in your future teaching?:

I will leave this module with the idea that this is a good strategy to implement early in the year through pod discussion so that students have more in their toolbox when it comes to writing analysis out.
