Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I really feel like they all go together! The notebook is a place to keep everything together and organized and something you can quickly revisit, go back to to gain ideas, expand, or revise thinking. The quicklist is a way to generate the ideas that so many folks who are reluctant writers need-because we just can't or don't seem to know where to begin. The writing sprints take the ideas and really begin to expand them, but the beautiful thing with the writing sprint is that there is an ending time!!! This is so important for folks who are weary with the whole writing thing! --

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

I deal with reluctant writers in intervention daily. The biggest challenge has been to get them excited and love writing. Using quicklists are a great way to begin as well as focusing in on personal connections to the writing--topics it is not very hard to generate ideas on. The easiest way to start would be to use the list exactly how it was presented to us in the module and accept and praise any approximation that the students are giving you!

I know you are ready to learn when we have our Writer’s Notebook and something to write with…

Open your notebook to the next clear page and write the date at the top.

Today we are going to create a quicklist.

Quicklists are used to help us generate our ideas!

We will be writing 10 things and will number our paper to 10.

I’ll read the prompts for you as we go-

1–2 List two things you did yesterday

3–4 Two things you like that others might not

5–6 Two conversations you have had recently (in-person or text/social media)

7–8 Two things you don’t like that others do

9-10 Two things you don’t think you could live without

We are going to look through our list and put a star next to 2-3 ideas that you think were your best ideas!
