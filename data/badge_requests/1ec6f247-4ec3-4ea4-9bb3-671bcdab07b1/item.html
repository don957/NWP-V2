Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

I learned that I need to provide more opportunities for my students to have conversations with each other. I feel that through the conversations they had their knowledge grew far more then if I had just stood up there and talked. I also learned that my students are extremely competitive and I can use that to get them more engaged with what they are doing. The research process allowed me to take the time to think about how my lesson is being beneficial and how it could be improved.
