Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

Take a Line for a Walk

This gives students a way to connect to a text and write what it makes them think of. It gives them a chance to write their thoughts without constraints using an excerpt from the text.

While working on our argumentative writing students have been forming their own claim adding or changing it with each source we read.
