How might you introduce Say-Mean-Matter to your students? What about this framework do you think will be helpful for them? What might be challenging?:

The Say-Mean- Matter procedure was introduced to my students as a way for them to be able to break down information that is provided to them so that it makes more sense. It helps them to take the time to look at material, think about what they notice and ask themselves what they wonder about the content. I really like that it leads the students to make connections between what they already know and what they still need to learn. I feel like it has been helpful to have the students slow down and make sense of what they are expected to do before they just jump in. Everything seems to be a little more challenging because many of our students are ELLs and the vocabulary really confuses them at times.
