Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

Looking back on this inquiry cycle, I learned that my teaching is more fast-paced than it should. After using these strategies, I learned that my students grasp the critical thinking that goes into their writing if we break apart texts and slow down to discuss. My classroom approach has changed, as now I will think about what I can do or what strategies I can use to have students think deeper about topic.

What other strategies are there that can be done within 10 minutes in the classroom that help the students think and write deeper? This is the main question I keep asking because these strategies are highly effective. The students enjoyed these strategies, so we will continue to use them.
