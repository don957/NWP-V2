Name: Makenzie Mudd
Badge: Joining a Conversation
What is one insight you gained from completing the Atwoodian Table yourself that might shape how you introduce it to students?:

I think that students could really be engaged by the idea that they will get to read some credible source information first before they form their personal summary. That could help some of our less confident students feel more prepared to share their thoughts.

How does the Atwoodian Table idea compare to how you have taught argument and/or responding to multiple texts in the past?:
In the past I have compared texts, but not in such a cohesive, visual way.
What do you notice about the two sets of excerpts in this module (children in fancy restaurants and toddlers and technology)? How do the excerpts work together to invite students to join the conversation? What are some conversations related to topics you teach?:
They have different overall focuses but come back to the idea of kids in technology. It is something I could use when introducing argumentative writing to show students that article titles may be deceiving and you might need to read further into a text to know if it is relevant for you or not.
