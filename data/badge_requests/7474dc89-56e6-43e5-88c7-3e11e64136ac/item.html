Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

Take a Line for a Walk is most usable in my classes since we are currently reading a couple of novels that have very thought provoking quotes. Taking the quotes - we can first analyze the meaning within the book and then relate it to today, to ourselves, our world right now. This can lead them along into thinking and sharing thoughts and writing about it before a discussion.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
Summaries are now what I have them do after chapter when reading novels in class. They are to read the chapter, write a summary, and then answer the questions. We are also using this routine to work on how to write summaries and to find key information.
