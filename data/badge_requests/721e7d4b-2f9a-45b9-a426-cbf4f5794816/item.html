Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

For practice, I took 'Line for a Walk' from the Atomic Habits. What resonated with me was 'the danger of aiming for perfection' - it had reminded me of Malcom Gladwell's 10,000 hours of practice to master complex skills. What I loved about the strategy was the ease at which you could start to write and write a lot on the topic while developing and expanding your connections to a quote. In the discussion my comments were really linked to the control it gave the students over their ideas and writing, the volume had actually increased for me when the prompt said, write where you mind takes you in regard to the this quote. There are so many directions you could go, and there is not right answer. We need our students to start writing quickly and to keep writing. This is very usable in the now.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
We have done some quick lists, write into the day prompts, I am wanting to try the clock writing for your day. Students are wanting to write quickly and be done. They have trouble generating ideas for their writing as well. The volume of their writing has been a challenge but- giving them choices, and giving them low stakes options as an entry has made all of the difference this year.
