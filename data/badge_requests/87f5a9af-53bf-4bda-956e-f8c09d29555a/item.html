Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I am already using something similar to "Writing Sprints" in my classroom. I like the Quicklists ideas though. They are less intimidating for middle schoolers to get some writing down and brainstorming going.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

I think feeling the need to grade it or check it is a challenge. Students wanting you to check their writing as well. It's a lot for a teacher. I remember hearing Kelly Gallagher speak on grading writing and how you have them write a lot but only grade or check over so often. I wish I could remember the details on that.
