Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

Take a line for a walk feel the most immediately usable because it honestly is the one I am most comfortable with. I found Interpreting Visual texts intimidating. However, since I do find it intimidating, I know it would be in my best interest to try to implement it in my classroom. Especially when I look at previous EOC tests and even our i-Ready diagnostic. Students are given audio or different, diverse text styles. Instead of shying away from the strategy that makes me nervous I should instead not avoid and get myself familiar with it. It was very helpful to look through others responses on participate that did do the visual text.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
For the most part students have been excited about writing in their writer's notebooks. The times I get push back is when they are too afriaid to "get it wrong." By continually reminding them this is low stakes, and you can't get it wrong, it definitly helps. But what if they are wrong. Like if they are taking a line for a walk but I truly can't understand their thought process. I have students that struggle so much with foundational skills just the idea of having to write a word freaks them out let alone any sustained writing. However the reminders for low stakes has helped thus far.
