Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

I learned that my students are hardwired for freedom and discussion when it is not graded but the minute I say something is graded or I attach a rubric, there is some rigidity in their thinking. Rubrics and grades are a part of my job so I've got to rework some things to make this process work for everyone. It was meaningful to reflect on each part of the process. I took a "let 'er rip" attitude and didn't provide a ton of sentence starters or thinking frames that I usually provide. I saw good and bad come from that.
