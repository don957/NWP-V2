Name: Hannah Mitchell
Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

I wrote about it in my last one but up until this lesson, research and argument analysis was information mining for my kids, no matter what I tried. This lesson shifted it to thinking about reading and writing as a conversation which improved engagement. I'm excited to start the year with this routine next year.

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
The discussions stemming from these texts actually came a lot more naturally than I anticipated they would. I didn't have to provide a ton of sentence starters and the conversations went lots of different directions which I thought was great. The biggest challenge was taking these rich discussions and turning them into complex writing. My students had a lot to say and their writing reflected that early on, but when it came to the final argument, the writing fell flat. I'm still trying to figure out why and how the complexity and nuance of their opinions was lost.
