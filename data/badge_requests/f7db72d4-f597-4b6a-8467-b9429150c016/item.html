Name: Stephanie Beadle
Badge: Everyday Sparks
Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I already use a Writer's Notebook in all of my classes, so I think I really want to try the quicklist and a writing sprint. We already have a writing territories page, so I think I will have them do a quicklist in their territories and then as our quick write that day, choose one of the things from their quicklist and do a writing sprint. I have been having them say "I'm done" before the time runs out, so this might help with that. I always tell them to keep writing anyway, but alas.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

I think they might have a hard time skipping things and coming back to them on the quick list. I also already have a hard time with them not wanting to write the full time on quick writes, so I'll be sure to phrase the writing sprint in a way that counteracts that. Something like, this is a sprint to the end. You have to keep our pencil moving the whole time and then stop when I say stop. I also have some personalities that will struggle with stopping and writing the ellipses.
