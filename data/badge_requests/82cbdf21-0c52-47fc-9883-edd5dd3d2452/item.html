Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

Take a Line for a Walk feels like it would easily be incorporated immediately. If I were teaching something and already had the resource made, all I would have to do is have the students select a line and respond. It feels very easy to incorporate.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
I've tried to have students free write just to get their thoughts down and to get used to spending time writing. The biggest challenge is having students write for an extended period of time.
