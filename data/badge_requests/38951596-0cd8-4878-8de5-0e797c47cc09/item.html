Name: Stephanie Bassman
Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

Revisiting a subject brings new reflection and discussion. The passing of time as they analyzed and reflected on previous readings and interviews showed how their knowledge of the subject increased and therefore changed opinions. Understanding their changes increased participation in writing. Seeing this will encourage ME to try this more often and in relation to novels we read.

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
I have 6 classes and therefore....6 different levels of discussion. Writing a response after reading or listening....then working in partners or groups before a general class discussion seems to help them bring ou t more thoughts and feelings. Does this always translate to more or better writing? Not always, BUT I see more thought, pauses, comments. I think that will lead to more discussion and writing as we continue.
