How might you introduce Say-Mean-Matter to your students? What about this framework do you think will be helpful for them? What might be challenging?:

I plan to introduce Say/Mean/Matter as soon as we begin our next reading unit. I think the section of 'mean' and 'matter' will be the most useful for them. The 'mean' forces students to break down what the text refers to and the general idea. The 'matter' makes them identify the relevance- which is so important in thoroughly understanding texts! Understanding why texts and quotes are relevant can be really challenging, I think this will be the biggest block for students. At times, my students struggle with not knowing how to word their thoughts, or how to get their thoughts from their brains to their assignment. I will have to use modeling techniques to help them with formulating their thoughts and opinions clearly and cohesively during the introduction stages of this strategy.
