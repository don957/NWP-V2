Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I found the Quicklists strategy to be the most immediately usable in my teaching context. I am working on teaching the cell division unit and we are focusing on mitosis. Students have been introduced to new vocabulary and concepts. A quicklists allows the students to write vocabulary or phrases they have heard of or remember what they mean. To extend that quicklist, the students can partner up and compare their lists, then organize the information into categories.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

I anticipate the biggest challenge to be students not understanding the need of writing within the science subject. I will address it by showing science articles written to share experiments or findings within the science field. This will help them see how sharing finding in science is important and vital in continuing research.
