Name: Janice Arnold
Badge: Everyday Sparks
Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I believe that the quicklists and writing sprints will be the most immediately usable in my classroom. My English I classes are getting ready to begin out personal narrative essays, and I often have students that have difficulty coming up with their topics. The personal narrative quicklist and writing sprint will be an excellent exercise to use as brainstorming exercises for their essays.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

I anticipate the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into the classroom is time. As I already have established lessons and curriculum and our class periods were shortened by the inclusion of an advisory period last year, time has become precious. I will have to find a way to amend our class schedule to fit writing into our everyday activities.
