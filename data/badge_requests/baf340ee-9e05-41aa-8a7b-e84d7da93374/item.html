Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I chose to use "Interpreting Visual Texts" because I work with students who are reluctant writers and am a resource class. I can't say enough about how difficult it is for me to add more load to my high school special education students; however, we must work on their writing abilities for their IEPs. I am trying to increase output as painlessly as possible. Therefore, I chose an infographic that most of my students would have a connection with.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
I have used the strategy "They say/I say" often during this school year. My biggest challenge is consistency with student mood, behavior, and attendance. I work with students who struggle with some extreme emotions and it truly depends on the mood of the room as to what we do for the day.
