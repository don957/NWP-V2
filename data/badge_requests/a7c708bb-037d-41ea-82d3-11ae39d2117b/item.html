Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

They Say/I Say During this activity, I noticed different foundational skills that need to be touched on during future meetings with my small title reading group. It was low-stakes writing, so I think that students wrote more than they would with just a writing prompt. The thing I loved about this activity was that my ELLs had a great discussion about what the text was saying. We did a lot of discussion before I had them write. I feel like this activity shaped my teaching because I plan to use my writing activities in my small reading groups.
