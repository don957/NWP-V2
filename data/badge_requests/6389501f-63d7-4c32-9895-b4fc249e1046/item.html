Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

I think my biggest surprise was that I can tie so much more discussions, writings, and graphics into math instruction. There are quite a few students who are limited English speaking in our school and they really struggle with making connections in their learning. We tried to really focus more on vocabulary development and writing about thinking. I will definitely continue to explore ways to incorporate more writing opportunities into my instruction.
