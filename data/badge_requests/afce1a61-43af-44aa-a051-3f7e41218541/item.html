Badge: Teacher as Researcher: Cultivating Literacy Routines
Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

After this cycle, I learned that a lot of the times, I am feeding things to my students instead of letting them explore and learn it on their own, with corrections from me. This definitely helped me get the idea that I needed to take a step back and let them take ownership of their learning, with guidance from the modules and with finding the connections they need for their individual lives.
