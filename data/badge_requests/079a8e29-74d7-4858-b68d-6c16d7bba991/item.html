Name: Isabella Orozco
Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

This gives students the opportunity to continuously shift their thoughts as they work through multiple perspectives and eventually create their own part of the conversation. Before, students might engage with one or two articles but they generally did not offer a variety of perspectives like this activity. I thought it was a really engaging activity that brought a lot of interesting ideas out in my students.

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
My students have really struggled with expressing opinions in class for fear of ridicule by their peers. We have been really working hard at habits of discourse all year. I believe that talking through ideas is the best way to prepare for writing. It gives students the ability to process out loud and with one another.
