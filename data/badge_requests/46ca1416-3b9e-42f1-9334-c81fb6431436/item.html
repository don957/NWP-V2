Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

My students are eager to please and learn. This process provided new insights into the way students conduct research for a writing task. I really enjoyed the whole aspect of the layering process and plan to continue this as a way to teache students how to conduct research for future projects. Sometimes it is easy to forget how frustrating a writing task can be when you do really know were to begin. This process is a great way for students to conduct research without becoming frustrated by the process.
