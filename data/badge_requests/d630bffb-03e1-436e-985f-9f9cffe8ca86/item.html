Badge: Everyday Sparks 2.0
Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

The strategy from this module that I see as most immediately usable is the use of visual texts. I have quite a few English language learners in my classes, and visual prompts work well with them. They are also easy to use during bellringers as quick writing prompts, ways to practice vocabulary, or ways to practice skills such as inferencing.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
As far as other approaches I have used, I have done quicklists at the start of units. They have been going okay. We have also done a lot of work with interpreting visual texts, such as graphs, infographics, and political cartoons. One challenge that I still face with my students is getting them to go from an idea that they have (whether it’s from a quicklist or something else they have come up with) and translating that into writing. A lot of my kids are constantly asking me how to start their writing, so we are trying not be so dependent on the teacher.
