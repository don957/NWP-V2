Name: Lindsey Wilson
Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

Previously in my classroom I have always gave the student two text on a topic, one that supported the topic and one that did not. After seeing this process I now know that I need to give my students more resources to see before they start their claim. I think this will help the students gain a better understanding of the topic and be able to see more as to why someone supports it or don't giving them more to think about when deciding what side of the argument they support.

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
The role of talk and discussion in my classrooms right now is not the best. Most students are to scared to share out their thoughts because they are not confident. Before anyone shares out loud in class I like for the students to share at their tables first so that they can hear what others have to say as well before being put on the spot. Students are also suppose to give feedback to each other, something positive and something that they can work on in a nice way of course. The only challenge is getting the students to feel comfortable to share with other students who they do not talk to on a daily basis.
