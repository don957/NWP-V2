How might you introduce Say-Mean-Matter to your students? What about this framework do you think will be helpful for them? What might be challenging?:

I used to use Say-Mean-Matter quite often in my earlier years of teaching and really liked it, but in the last four years, I started using the CLEAR (Claim, Lead-in, Evidence, Additional Knowledge, and Reasoning) set up. I liked Say, Mean, Matter for breaking down a quote, but I have found I prefer CLEAR when it comes to teaching kids how to build strong body paragraphs. I find they are both very similar in strategy, but CLEAR helps them focus on what all good paragraphs should have. However, I think Say-Mean-Matter could be good for a quick pod discussion where I didn't have to have them produce the body paragraph. I could also have use Say-Mean-Matter in their groups to then move them towards the CLEAR process.

As far as my uploaded examples, I would use the graphic organizer provided in the module for the pod activity. As far as where I can use this, we could easily do this on Monday. Tuesday before Thanksgiving Break, my English II students went to the Holocaust museum. On Monday, I was going to ask them to respond to one of the quotes in the attachment below in reflecting about their experience at the Holocaust museum. This is a great way to quickly do that with their pod before we move into their museum artifact four modes of writing assignment.

Because my students are pretty familiar with CLEAR at this point, I do not anticipate this activity will be too challenging for them, especially while working with their pods. For my students who are still struggling with analysis, I think this will be helpful for them because it will be another way for them to consider how to build strong analysis around evidence.
