Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

The writer's notebook feels the most immediately usable. This is the place where my students are able to be creative and keep all their work. I find it important that they only have one place to put all of their class work, because they struggle to keep track of everything. The kids have really like being able to make them their own.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

Writer fatigue seems to be the biggest challenge of of writing everyday. Throughout the year we have worked on increasing the amount of time spent on writing. Working in SPED my kids always struggle to work on hard things or for extended periods of times. In order to meet this challenge I work to on breaking up assignments to help with them getting tired.
