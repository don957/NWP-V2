Name: Stacey Kay
Badge: Reading to Write: They Say / I Say
Which version of the They Say / I Say routine (with visual texts or with annotation) feels like the best fit for your students right now? Why?:

Annotations seem best suited for my students at this time because they read such a wide variety of texts. I also feel like the annotations help spark meaningful academic conversations.

Describe one way you might adapt the They Say / I Say framework to align with the types of texts your students read most often (e.g., articles, visuals, multimodal texts).:

Since my students most frequently read research articles, I can see myself selecting at least one broad article for a group of students to read based on their research theme. This could be a springboard for individualizing research topics and establishing a common academic vocabulary.

What, if anything, do you need help thinking through as you plan for using They Say / I Say in your classroom?:

I believe I have used a version of this for years; therefore, I don't think I need any additional support.
