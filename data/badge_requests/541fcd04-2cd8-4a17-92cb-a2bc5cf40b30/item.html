How might you introduce Say-Mean-Matter to your students? What about this framework do you think will be helpful for them? What might be challenging?:

I have selected a quote about expectations and how students live up - or down - to them. I think by selecting a quote that's not necessarily "science-heavy" or abstract it will help them to be less intimidated by it and perhaps they'll put more effort into the activity. Most of our students just do not seem to have a growth mindset and they're very apathetic about everything, so they tend to balk any time they're asked to actually *think*. I'm very hopeful that by utilizing some of these strategies we're learning about we can start to turn that corner and help our kids see that HERE is not where they have to stay; they have so much potential and I'd love to see them put in some work to get there!!
