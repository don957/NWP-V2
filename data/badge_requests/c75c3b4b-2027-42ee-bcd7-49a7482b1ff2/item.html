Name: Kayla Peterman
Badge: Teacher as Researcher: Cultivating Literacy Routines
Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

I learned from teaching the Say, Mean, Matter style that I can get students to think deeply about a certain piece of work just by breaking it down for them and guiding them as to how I want their minds to look at something. Using a different lens is easier to teach than I originally thought it would be. I also used this method during a classroom observation, and my observer was amazed at how well the students did with this and encouraged me to share it with my fellow teachers.

Share one artifact or anecdote from trying out your chosen strategy (e.g., a piece of student work, notes from your Writer’s Notebook, a classroom snapshot, or a brief story from the experience).:
What new questions or next steps are you leaving this module with? How might you continue to explore or build on this inquiry in your future teaching?:

I am going to continue to look for text for students to analyze. I am considering using this method when we end a novel and discuss the theme of the book. Then, once the theme is established, we can do the Say, Mean, Matter method to analyze what the author is trying to get across to the reader.
