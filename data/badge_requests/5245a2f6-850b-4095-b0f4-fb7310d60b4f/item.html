Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

I learned that teaching allows for creativity there is room to explore and try new writing styles. You do not have to follow the R.A.C.E paragraph framework all the time. I do find benefit in the R.A.C.E. format, but there is so many more way to express thought and ideas beyond this framework. Going through the teacher-research process allowed me to try new things and process with other on how they worked. My students through trying new strategies of writing have grown to enjoy write. There is a joy when we get out our writer notebooks, because students are able to learn but in a fun and free way.
