Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

Using the quick write strategy would be immediately usable in my teaching context. Students are given a topic to write about. They can write non stop without correcting errors for a set amount of time. Once complete doors are open for discussion, reflections, and corrections.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
They say, I say has been used this year. Using this strategy gives students a clear starting point. Students first need to understand, then respond.
