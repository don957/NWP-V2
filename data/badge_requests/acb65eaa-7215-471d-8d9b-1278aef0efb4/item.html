How might you introduce Say-Mean-Matter to your students? What about this framework do you think will be helpful for them? What might be challenging?:

I would introduce the Say–Mean–Matter strategy by using an organizer. First, I would explain that “Say” is what the text literally states, “Mean” is what the text implies or explains in the student’s own words, and “Matter” is why the information is important or how it connects to a bigger idea. The most challenging part in my classroom will be clarifying the "Matter" part.
