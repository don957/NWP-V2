Badge: Everyday Sparks
Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

Writing Sprints feels most immediately usable, probably because I already like using them. We do a district wide writing assessment where the students must write a short constructed response in twelve minutes based off of a prompt. The goal was for them to get 200 words hand written. When I first started teaching our writing stamina was a joke. So someone through Northwest RPDC told me about writer's notebooks and writing sprints. I then had my students chart their words per minute. I went from students not being able to write for a whole 12 minutes for them being mad that 12 minutes is too short of time.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

Me remembering. When I want to use things like quicklists in class I forget to go back to the quicklist. I had great intentions to write throughout the day but then struggle to remember to revisit our writing.
