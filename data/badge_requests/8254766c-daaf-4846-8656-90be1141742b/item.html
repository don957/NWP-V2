How might you introduce Say-Mean-Matter to your students? What about this framework do you think will be helpful for them? What might be challenging?:

Say-Mean-Matter could be applied to so many things: famous quotes (from scientists), quotes/excerpts from articles, video clips, TED talks, graphs and infographics, etc. I would introduce this with something smaller, like a quote or a graph. I think the framework is excellent for helping to scaffold their thinking from paraphrasing, to meaning making, to reflection/analysis/application. I think it may be most challenging for students to determine why these things matter but that's also the most important step of all. And it's good to be challenged!
