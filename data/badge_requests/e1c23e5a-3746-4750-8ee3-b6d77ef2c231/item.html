Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I actually like all of these strategies and think they would be great to implement within the classroom. I am a visual learner and know that about myself. So my first choice would be to include visual texts to help various learners to feel like they understand the material and can contribute within the classroom. I have a special education background so I love the idea of different pictures for different kids also so it is not as obvious that there is an accommodation in play. I think its powerful to have more than one option for the whole class so no one knows which gets are getting "easier" visuals. This allows everyone to be a learner and make their own connections and learn from what they are seeing visually. Even if that means they are just learning how to read the visual. For the module I chose the Take a Line for a Walk because I think this strategy helps students to gain confidence in what they know and learning from how they process information. Getting students to try and be willing to share their own ideas is key when trying to build student agency within the classroom.
