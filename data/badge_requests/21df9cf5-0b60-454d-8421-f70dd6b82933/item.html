Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I really like the idea of making a quicklist of all the stakeholders around a particular issue. For example, building a dam across a river to create a lake. There are so many people who would be affected by this, in various ways. Having students brainstorm a list of those who may be affected could be really interesting. Then, they could revisit this list to dig into how they are affected. I just recently did a presentation around drone use and I selected the stakeholders (lens) to use. However, I think this would be a really good pre-writing prior to that activity.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

A brief note about any challenges you anticipate and how you might address them.

I think setting up any regular writing routine can be a challenge. As a science teacher, we read and write in so many different ways. I must be intentional when I am planning my instruction and not skip these important routines because of time.
