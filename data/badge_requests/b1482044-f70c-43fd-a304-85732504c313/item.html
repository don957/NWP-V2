Badge: Joining a Conversation
What is one insight you gained from completing the Atwoodian Table yourself that might shape how you introduce it to students?:

I believe that the concept of the table and a space to represent each person's thoughts/ideas will be really powerful for my students when it comes to keeping track of different perspective and taking all perspectives into account. Graphic organizers are helpful for most students!

How does the Atwoodian Table idea compare to how you have taught argument and/or responding to multiple texts in the past?:
I have typically had student only look at research or other perspectives that match their own claims and ideas. This idea has opened my eyes to see that students need to review all sides of an "argument" before making their own claim. I feel that this will also help make their counter-claims and rebuttals stronger.
What do you notice about the two sets of excerpts in this module (children in fancy restaurants and toddlers and technology)? How do the excerpts work together to invite students to join the conversation? What are some conversations related to topics you teach?:
Both of these concepts deal with children, so students are more apt to relate to the ideas. The excerpts were easy to read and follow. I allow students to choose from a wide range of topics for their writing. Most of what I use is similar to these topics in the fact that they are what children are "allowed" to do: Should middle school students have recess? Should students have to wear uniforms? Phones in school, etc.
