Literacy Routines

Certificate

Issuer
National Writing Project

Description

This credential recognizes full participation in the Literacy th@t Works fall and spring online course modules. By completing all modules, earning each badge and microcredential, and engaging in reflection and discussion, you have demonstrated a sustained commitment to literacy routines and practicing teacher inquiry. This credential signifies your accomplishment in completing a full year of coursework and your readiness to carry these practices forward into your classroom and future professional learning.

Criteria

Earn the Cultivating Literacy Routines and Sustaining Literacy Routines credentials:

  • Cultivating Literacy Routines

  • Sustaining Literacy Routines
