Description

This badge recognizes your participation in the three-part discussion cycle for this module. By posting and responding in each stage—before, during, and after teaching—you contribute to a shared inquiry process where teachers learn from one another’s experiences. Together, these conversations help build a supportive community of teacher-researchers.

Criteria

To earn the discussion badge for this module, be sure to:

  • Post in all three discussions (Preparing to Teach, During Teaching, and After Teaching).

  • Respond to other teachers in each discussion to keep the conversation going.
