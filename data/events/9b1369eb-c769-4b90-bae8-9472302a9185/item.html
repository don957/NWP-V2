About Event

This 1 hour real-time Zoom event will bring together new and returning teachers who are participating in the Missouri Show Me Literacies Collaborative (SMLC) program. During our time together, we will connect to continue building our online learning community, explore the overall approach to teaching argument writing for the courses, and further orient ourselves to the courses and the Participate platform.

There will also be time in breakout discussions for participants to explore the plan for the course series they are participating in, including the timeline, processes, and learning opportunities for the year.

We are looking forward to seeing you all for the launch of the 2025-26 SMLC online courses!
