What is one insight you gained from completing the Atwoodian Table yourself that might shape how you introduce it to students?:

I think that I would go about introducing this with student in relation to arguments they might have had with others. This would help them draw on some background information about differing point of views. We would talk about looking at the other persons thoughts and trying to understand them, while also supporting our own.

How does the Atwoodian Table idea compare to how you have taught argument and/or responding to multiple texts in the past?:
I have not really gotten to much into argumentative writing, but I would use this as an introductory lesson.
What do you notice about the two sets of excerpts in this module (children in fancy restaurants and toddlers and technology)? How do the excerpts work together to invite students to join the conversation? What are some conversations related to topics you teach?:
The excerpts gave two differing sides of an argument, and between the two sources for each there were different examples of ways to support each side. This really provides reluctant kids with different options that might relate to their thoughts.
