Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I want to do take a line for a walk with my students. They can do this from any of the layered writing pieces we are using or the curriculum that our school has. Not only do the students get to take charge and state their own opinion, it can be done in a low pressure way. This gets them to connect more with what they are reading.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
I have a challenge getting students to write more. I have used visuals, free write, and beginning prompts and all of them work. the students just don't write as much as I know they can.
