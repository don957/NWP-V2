I learned that I am not teaching to my full potential and as a result I am not expecting the students best in return. I also need to remember that it is my job to teach scientific literacy and I have not been as intentional as I need to be with that. I need to do like you mentioned and pick a day of the week for writing as a starting point in all of my classes.
