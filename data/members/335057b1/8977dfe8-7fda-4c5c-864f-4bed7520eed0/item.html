Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

A strategy from the module that I plan to try next is the quicklist. I think this approach will really help my students get their thoughts flowing instead of simply sitting and waiting for inspiration to strike.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

Possible challenges I anticipate include:

A student may recall a difficult memory or thought, which could bring up old emotions. I’ve experienced this before with narrative writing, and I always make sure students know that resources are available if they ever want to talk.

Some students may still say, “I don’t know what to write,” even after completing a quicklist. In those cases, I try to make time to sit down with them one-on-one and help them brainstorm a topic.
