Which version of the They Say / I Say routine (with visual texts or with annotation) feels like the best fit for your students right now? Why?:

The version of the They Say / I Say routine would be the visual texts. With ELL students, reading is not always easy, and they don't always understand what they have read. But with the visual texts, it is easier to identify the message and can comment according to how they feel about it.

Describe one way you might adapt the They Say / I Say framework to align with the types of texts your students read most often (e.g., articles, visuals, multimodal texts).:

Adapting the They Say / I Say framework to align with my students would mean modifying the text to a level more suited to learners' reading ability. This would be easy to accomplish with the aid of AI. This would allow my students to participate with more confidence in what they are asked to do.
