Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

The strategy from this module that feels most immediately usable in my teaching context is Interpreting Visual Texts. This strategy works well for ELL students. They usually like using visuals more than using just text. Sometimes, I will need to help them interpret graphs and other parts of the infographics. As they interpret the infographic, they can discuss what they learn from the material, and that helps them with English, too.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
I used the Quick Write, Writers Notebook, and Say Mean Matter with my ELL students. The ELL students generally do not like writing, no matter what strategies I use. They struggle with using the right words to express their thoughts and even getting their thoughts on paper. They will use as few words as possible to write and they complain a lot!
