Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

I think actively reflecting on how our students engage with different strategies helps us to identify the most effective tools to bring/keep in our practices. I try to be a reflective practitioner, and it comes easily for the most part; however, it has been nice to be a little more intentional with these strategies. Moving forward, I realize that my younger students use the strategies better and more effectively. Perhaps this is because they are more apt to just do the things I ask them to do!

All of my 7th graders did a solid job of creating good lists and free-writing during the sprints.
