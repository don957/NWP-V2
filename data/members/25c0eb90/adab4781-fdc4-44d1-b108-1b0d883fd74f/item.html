How might you introduce Say-Mean-Matter to your students? What about this framework do you think will be helpful for them? What might be challenging?:

To introduce Say-Mean-Matter to my students, I think I might start by posting a quote from an article or story we will be taking a look at within a specific unit. Then, I would simply ask, "What does this quote mean?" Based on my past experiences, this usually results in a lot of silence with only a few students offering a comment. I would then move into introducing the concept of Say-Mean-Matter to the students.

With this framework, it gives the students a structure through which they can view the quote. They start at the surface level by simply taking in the actual quote, and then gradually uncover the deeper meaning by exploring what the quote means and why it matters.

As for challenges, I could see my students struggling to differentiate between the "mean" and "matter" components. A lot of them will probably think that the meaning of the quote would automatically tell you why the quote itself matters.
