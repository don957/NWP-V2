Badge: Reading to Write: They Say / I Say
Which version of the They Say / I Say routine (with visual texts or with annotation) feels like the best fit for your students right now? Why?:

The annotation version of They say / I say feels like the best fit for my students right now because annotating has been my focus for my students. I also value breaking up reading into smaller chunks. My students love having conversations, so I also like that this encourages them to converse with the text.

Describe one way you might adapt the They Say / I Say framework to align with the types of texts your students read most often (e.g., articles, visuals, multimodal texts).:

My students mostly read articles but they also watch videos. This could be adapted by making the sections of reading gradually longer so that it can help students build reading stamina.
