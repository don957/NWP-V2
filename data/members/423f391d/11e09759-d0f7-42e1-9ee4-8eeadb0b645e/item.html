Badge: Reading to Write: They Say / I Say
Which version of the They Say / I Say routine (with visual texts or with annotation) feels like the best fit for your students right now? Why?:

We are just beginning to construct and write essays. I plan to use the They Say/I Say annotation of informative texts with my students. I want them to feel at ease with this type of text in preparation for the EOC writing portion.

Describe one way you might adapt the They Say / I Say framework to align with the types of texts your students read most often (e.g., articles, visuals, multimodal texts).:

I plan to adapt the annotations of written texts as a way to assist in creating an outline for an argumentative essay. We will use informative texts/articles for this. The students will have two pieces of text, and they will create their outline after annotating both.

What, if anything, do you need help thinking through as you plan for using They Say / I Say in your classroom?:

I want to select pieces of text that will lend themselves to success in creating the outline. Students need to be able to make their own claim, defend it, and cite evidence from the two pieces of text that they are given.
