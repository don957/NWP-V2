Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I think either the daily schedule or the interpreting visual texts feels most immediately usable in my content. I think the visual texts would be simple to implement because we could use graphs in science or other visual texts to practice CER or start discussions about real world events happening and relate it to the material we are learning in Biology. The topic that comes to mind when I look at the daily schedule is homeostasis. They could make the graph and we could talk about ways that their body regulates homeostasis during the activities that are working on.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
I've used some MRI strategies that I've learned. Such as, "Now I'm thinking" which I feel is similar to the layered writing but not as in depth. I've also used Iron chef when we were talking about ecology. I also used the pictures we got from the conference and had them do the same writing activity that we did at the conference. I think the challenges that I've faced are just the complaints about having to write and sometimes they aren't engaged with the text which makes it hard to continue the strategy.
