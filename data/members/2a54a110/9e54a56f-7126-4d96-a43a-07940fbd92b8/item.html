Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

I think The Say Mean Matter strategy is applicable to all areas of school. I think with lots of practice students are able to "allow" themselves to write what they are thinking without being worried they are not writing the "correct" things. I choose to introduce this strategy with my students in more of a non academic way, but more of an individual goal setting self reflection piece. I think this allowed some of my lower writers to jump in and make personal connections. I would like to revisit this after the new year and provide specific feedback for each response. After the feedback with each student, the hope would be that the student can now make an action plan for the matter portion of this strategy. Students have already recognized why this mattered to them, now the hope is to help them think about what steps they can take to achieve or work towards making this apply to "their matter". My hope is that it would bring in the agency from the student to want to take the next steps to help them become better students in they way they identified that mattered to them.
