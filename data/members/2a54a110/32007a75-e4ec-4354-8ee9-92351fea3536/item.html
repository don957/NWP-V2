What is one insight you gained from completing the Atwoodian Table yourself that might shape how you introduce it to students?:

I think the power of perspective is important and many students today struggle to see other points of view. I think the Atwoodian Table helps students to stop and think from one else's perspective. I think the more students are able to open their minds to others thinking, the more powerful their mind becomes. Our society needs growth mindset individuals as opposed to closed minded and my way or the high way.

How does the Atwoodian Table idea compare to how you have taught argument and/or responding to multiple texts in the past?:
I think argumentative texts allows you to choose a side and defend it. I think Atwoodian Table makes you focus on how different occupants at the table might have a different view positive or negative.
