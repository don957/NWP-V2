What is one insight you gained from completing the Atwoodian Table yourself that might shape how you introduce it to students?:

I believe this method will teach students to keep an open mind until all points have been heard. I am going to stress that sometimes we make up our minds too quickly and don't really examine other perspectives on the subject. I will have my students use this method when they begin research on their fourth quarter argumentative essay. This is will be something they will complete, turn in in advance, and then we will discuss one on one their findings and how they will approach their paper.

How does the Atwoodian Table idea compare to how you have taught argument and/or responding to multiple texts in the past?:
It provides a visual of the various opinions on a specific topic. This is a completely different way than I have taught research for the arugumentaive paper in the past.
