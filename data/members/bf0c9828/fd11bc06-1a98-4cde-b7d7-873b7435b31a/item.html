Name: Katie Cornine
Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

I have not had a lot of experience working with middle schoolers on any kind of writing, just in the elementary setting. When thinking back to previous experience as an elementary writing teacher, I would give a prompt and not support it with a text. This process has helped me realize the importance of using multiple texts and seeing all sides of the conversation. This process can be integrated easily with what I have learned in Lexia Aspire training. This process will help me easily guide my students through making a claim about a topic.

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
Before implementing this process, we didn't have a lot of discussion in my small reading groups. We talked about text we are reading. Students have the opportunity in small reading groups to discuss their thoughts related to the text. I have used Lexia Aspire forms to help guide my students this year. The layered reading, writing, and discussion process get students engaged in all 3. While implementing this process, some students were reluctant to share their thoughts. When one student spoke up about what they highlighted others would get excited when they agreed. They eventually got more engaged in the process as we moved along through the different texts. I think they are so used to is it right or wrong that they are reluctant to share their thoughts. As they are exposed more to this process, I think they will get more comfortable with sharing their ideas even when they are opposing.
