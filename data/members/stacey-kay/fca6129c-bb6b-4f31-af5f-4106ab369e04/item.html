Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

In all sincerity, I had never heard of the say/mean/matter notetaking method before, and after teaching my students the process, their argumentation essays are so much better because I had the language and tool to help them break down a complicated process. Previously, I used an ACE (answer, cite, explain) for responding to constructed response questions with my underclassmen, especially when responding to literature, but that was not working for college composition. This strategy helped me teach a process that was innate to me.
