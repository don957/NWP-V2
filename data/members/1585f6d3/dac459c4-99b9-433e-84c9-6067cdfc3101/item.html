How might you introduce Say-Mean-Matter to your students? What about this framework do you think will be helpful for them? What might be challenging?:

I think that introducing this strategy to my students would be easiest by using the quotes on the Google Slides from the modules. Each of the quotes included has a deeper meaning that could be easy for students to think through and identify. At first, I might allow students to do this with a partner in order to give them someone to reflect on their thoughts with. I think the prompt questions on the routine template with be extremely helpful for students. The challenging part for them might be that some of them often wait to be told what to think and don't feel confident "free thinking" for themselves.

As a side note, the sample that I am uploading below will be an introduction activity for my students. They are 6th graders and have not been asked to think outside of the box very often in their academic careers. After using this sample with them, I will create similar activities with other choses parts of texts we are reading, and eventually work up to using the same chart, but allowing them to choose the part of the text to analyze.
