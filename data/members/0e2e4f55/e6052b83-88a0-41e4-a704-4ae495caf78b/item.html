Which strategy from this module (Daily Schedule, Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I think Take a Line for a Walk or Interpreting Visual Texts feel immediately usable in my teaching context. Specifically with Interpreting Visual Texts, I feel like I could use some of the links provided to have a daily visual for students to interpret/write about as a bell ringer activity (or at least a couple of times a week). I could incorporate an image/chart/infographic, etc. into my daily slideshow for each class and students could write their responses to it while everyone is getting settled in, then occasionally we could have partner, small group, or class discussion about their interpretations.

What other strategies or approaches have you used to engage your students in routine writing over the course of this school year? What, if any, challenges have you encountered in engaging students in sustained routine writing?:
The only strategy I've used fairly regularly thus far is the writer's notebooks. Our students aren't accustomed to doing much writing, having to answer in full sentences, restating the question, or being held accountable for doing GOOD work, so it's a process. That said, I feel like we, as a team, are all on the same page and we're all working collectively to improve outcomes for our kids.
