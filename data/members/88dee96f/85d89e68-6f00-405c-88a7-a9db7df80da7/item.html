What is one insight you gained from completing the Atwoodian Table yourself that might shape how you introduce it to students?:

I love the visual element of it. I will present it as another type of annotation, or tracking our thinking during reading.

How does the Atwoodian Table idea compare to how you have taught argument and/or responding to multiple texts in the past?:
It is different because of the visual aspect and the conciseness. I have used MPOs from TWR in the past. And I do like those, but this is a shorter way to get some kernels down on paper and annotate.
What do you notice about the two sets of excerpts in this module (children in fancy restaurants and toddlers and technology)? How do the excerpts work together to invite students to join the conversation? What are some conversations related to topics you teach?:
Both texts are familiar to students, or could impact students. I like using topics geared toward their lives to get engagement. I will use some of those topics. However, our primary focus is the text we are reading for the unit. I will have to pace accordingly.
