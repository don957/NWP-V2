Name: Cathy Rahder
Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

I have always taught argumentative writing using the 5 paragraph style. I know many people say this is not effective, but I disagree. With 6th graders, getting them to put their thoughts on paper is hard. They have only been introduced to opinion papers and not arguments. Therefore, I won't change that aspect of how I teach argumentative writing. What I will change is using this graphic organizer to begin the process. This strategy really shows them both sides of the story before they begin to write. That is critical. It will change how they approach their argument. This broadens their horizons, expands their thinking, and forces them to see the other side FIRST!

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
Talk and discussion is how I lead my classroom. My students are very familiar with Socratic Seminars for every topic we begin. I have tried every strategy I have learned throught this year course, and I plan on using each one of them from here on out. I absolutey loved this graphic organizer, but I am still partial to the Attwodian Table strategy.
