Name: Lisa Pruett
Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

We don't typically do argument writing in my science classroom. This is new to me, but I hope to integrate more writing in my curriculum for next year. My students struggle with writing and sometimes even comprehending the purpose of a text. I know they will improve as I learn more.

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
My students are active participants in our daily discussions. I use multiple strategies to give students opportunities to share their ideas in small groups or with a partner. We use jigsaw, back to back/face to face and take one/get one/switch, to name a few. I think overall, the students struggle putting their thoughts on paper in a way that can be understood by the reader.
