How might you introduce Say-Mean-Matter to your students? What about this framework do you think will be helpful for them? What might be challenging?:

I think I would introduce it like the slideshow provided here did. I would put up a quote, give the students a post it note and on the front the note they would write what they think the quote means and then flip the post-it and write why it matters on the back. I think doing it this way will be helpful because a post-it is small so it won't be too overwhelming for them to try. It might be challenging to get some kids to stop and think and actually write some. I have some students that really don't want to do their own thinking- I would need to try and find a good quote to inspire them to start.
