What is one insight you gained from completing the Atwoodian Table yourself that might shape how you introduce it to students?:

I teach special education and I like that this can be used to show more than one viewpoint. I also like that the student can add their argument to the table and can see all aspects of an argument.

How does the Atwoodian Table idea compare to how you have taught argument and/or responding to multiple texts in the past?:
It helps to have the visual instead of a bunch of sources the students have to read through. I would start by giving them the Atwoodian table and later have them create their own.
What do you notice about the two sets of excerpts in this module (children in fancy restaurants and toddlers and technology)? How do the excerpts work together to invite students to join the conversation? What are some conversations related to topics you teach?:
Most of my students have been using technology since they were toddlers and they can relate to the topic on an honest level. It also gives them the freedom to speak their part on how technology is affecting youth.
