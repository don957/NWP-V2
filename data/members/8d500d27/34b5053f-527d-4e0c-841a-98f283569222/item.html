Name: Chrystal Pratt
Badge: Layering Reading, Writing and Thinking
How does the Layering Reading, Writing, and Discussion approach compare to how you have previously supported students in engaging with multiple texts or teaching argument writing? How might this process shift or refine the way you guide students toward forming their own claims?:

This is the first time I have used layered reading and writing to teach argumentative writing. Teaching special education, argumentative writing, I have always taught as an interest-based approach. Students write about what they know and interests them. There may be times in their high school career where arguments need cited and are fact-based instead of just opinionated. They really struggled with forming their own claims and the whole instruction was very uided.

How would you describe the role of talk and discussion in your classroom right now? What kinds of opportunities do students have to rehearse ideas through conversation? What challenges have you encountered in making talk a meaningful part of the writing process?:
We have a very open-discussion type of classroom where all opinions are valued, even if we disagree. We discuss even touchy subjects like politics without students getting heated or finger pointing. My biggest challenge has been getting students to form their own ideas. Like I mentioned before this has all been very guided for them.
