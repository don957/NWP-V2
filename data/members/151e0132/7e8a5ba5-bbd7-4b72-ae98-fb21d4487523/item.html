Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

I learned that my students can enjoy writing. Our writing block is at the end of the day and they always huff and puff when it is that time. I have tried various skills and techniques, they are just done by then. However, I never once heard a complaint from them when it was time to work on this lesson. This process has made me start to think that if I was able to choose topics/lessons that were this interesting and relatable to them, would I have more engagement?

With the first lesson, students watched the video, took notes, and then had a discussion with a partner. What stood out to me was how productively engaged they were with their discussion. I have been working with them to have deeper discussions, rather than just surface-level sharing with their partners. By the end of the discussions, they were able to write down additional notes that they gained from their parnter. This reveals that they are able to have deeper, productive discussions with each other.
