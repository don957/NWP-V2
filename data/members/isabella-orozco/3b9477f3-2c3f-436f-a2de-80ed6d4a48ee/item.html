Looking back on this inquiry cycle, what did you learn about your teaching and your students that you might not have noticed otherwise? How did engaging in this teacher-research process shape or shift your thinking about your classroom practice?:

I think that this was a great way to help me push students to become less than perfect writers. Too often we are worried about making sure they get it right, rather than the messiness and beauty that comes from just trying and doing! My students especially have struggled with the fear of "being wrong" or "doing it wrong" so much so that it impacts their ability to even try. Through this process, I have seen so much more creativity and openness that I ever would have seen before!
