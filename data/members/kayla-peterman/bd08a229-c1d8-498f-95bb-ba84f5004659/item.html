Which strategy from this module (Writer’s Notebook, Quicklists, or Writing Sprints) feels most immediately usable in your teaching context? Why?:

I think the Writing Sprints strategy would be the most immediately usable for my students. I think that taking the pressure off of students to write freely and not geared so specifically to a certain topic will help them enjoy the writing process a little better.

What do you anticipate will be the biggest challenge in bringing daily writing into your classroom, and how might you address it?:

I think my biggest challenge would be to get the students to writing for 2-3 minutes without stopping. A lot of times students will get a writers block and just stop writing. I also have students that don't like writing so they may write one sentence and thing that is sufficient. I will encourage them to writing a thought provoking question about their topic so that it may spark another idea for writing.
