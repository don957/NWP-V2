How might you introduce Say-Mean-Matter to your students? What about this framework do you think will be helpful for them? What might be challenging?:

I would introduce the Say-Mean-Matter framework by modeling it with a short, text or quote that pertains to our history subject. First, I would read the quote aloud with the class and then think through each step out loud: what the author says, what it means, and why it matters. I would also provide sentence starters and examples so students have a structure to follow. I think this framework will be helpful because it gives students a clear way to move beyond surface-level reading. Many students can tell me what a text says, but they struggle to explain its meaning. One of the challenges will be helping students understand the difference between Mean and Matter.
