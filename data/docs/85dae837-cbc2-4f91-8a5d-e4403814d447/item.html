Course Orientation

Welcome to the National Writing Project’s SMLC Source-Based Argument course series for 2024-25. This National Writing Project yearlong course is designed to support teachers in the Missouri Show Me Literacies Collaborative.

Over the course of the 2024-25 school year, you will examine your own teaching practices, experience high-leverage instructional approaches, and engage with practical and strategic applications for the classroom—all focused on the specific teaching of source-based argument writing.

As is the case with all courses in the Teaching Argument Writing community, this course series was developed based on the research and design of NWP’s College, Career, and Community Writer’s Program (C3WP), a program that has been proven to have a statistically significant impact on students’ argument writing in three randomized-control trials.

Beginning in August 2025, this new course series will follow the 2025-26 school year and is designed for classroom teachers in grades 3-12, including academic coaches, support staff, administrators, and professional development providers. It’s perfect for anyone who wants to learn more about argument writing instruction!

What You Will Get

  • A state-wide community of educators interested in a deep exploration of source-based argument writing.

  • A dedicated Coach/Thinking Partner from the National Writing Project who will support you as you plan for integrating source-based argument writing in your existing curriculum

  • A synchronous virtual launch that will include community building, a course orientation, and an overview of this approach to source-based argument instruction

  • Support for creating new argument writing activities for your students and teaching context

  • Two lesson studies (fall and spring) that will support you in planning for, teaching, and reflecting on the source-based argument instructional resources

  • A synchronous virtual mid-year workshop

  • A digital credential to signify the completion of each section of the course (35 learning hours total)

Coach/Thinking Partner

This course will be facilitated by National Writing Project teacher-leaders who have practical experience in teaching argument writing in their own classrooms, and in providing online professional development for teachers. Your Coach/Thinking Partner will provide you with individualized feedback on your coursework.

Every submission is an opportunity for feedback and reflection. Engage in a two-way discussion with your Thinking Partner to gain deeper insights. Revise and improve your submissions based on the feedback.

Earn Digital Credentials

Completing coursework allows you to earn verified digital credentials that recognize your learning, skills, and professional development.

These credentials document your expertise, can be shared on your profile and LinkedIn, and may even count toward continuing education units (CEUs) in some districts. 🚀

Course Schedule

In the beginning of each semester, you are invited to attend a virtual event. In the summer of 2024 you will be given three options for a 2-hour event (August 13th at 4pm CT, 15th at 4pm CT or 17th at 10am CT). In January 2024 the 105 minute event is on January 15th at 4pm CT.

In each semester there will be one course (4 weeks in the fall and 2 weeks in the spring). You will be invited to try out new instructional approaches, discuss and share with other course participants, and reflect on your own teaching practice and experiences. Your Coach/Thinking Partner will be there to support you every step of the way!

Following each course is a lesson study during which you will be invited to try out a lesson sequence in your classroom.
