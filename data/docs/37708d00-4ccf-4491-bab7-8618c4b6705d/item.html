Lesson Study

We are excited to share this experience with you. Some of you have taught this lesson sequence, while others will teach it during the 3 weeks or even later this year. We look forward to having all of these perspectives represented as we study the Connecting Evidence with Claims lesson sequence together.

By Lil_Foot on Pixabay

Each module is an invitation to reflect on your plans for and/or experiences with teaching the Connecting with Claims Lesson Sequence. As you post responses on your discussion group, please also reply to each other offering appreciation, asking questions, and adding new ideas that spark discussion.

  • If you are teaching the lesson sequence now or have already taught it, we want to hear all about how your learners respond to the activities. What works for them? What surprises you? What questions come up?

  • If you are teaching it later this year, anticipate and plan for the application of the activities with your learners. This might look like you trying out the activities yourself and responding to the prompts from your own perspective as a learner, and thinking through how your learners will respond to the activities. What might work for your learners? What might surprise you? What questions do you have?

We will ask common reflective questions throughout the study so that you can respond as the learning is happening within and across the lessons.

Please know that you do not need to answer every reflection question every time. We would like to invite you to reflect on the questions that best fit where you and your learners are in this work.

Please let us know your perspective as a participant in the lesson study and share your response in the discussion. Are you joining us already teaching the lesson sequence, are you teaching the lesson sequence during these 3 weeks, or will you be planning to teach the lesson sequence at another time this year?

Planning for Lesson Study Implementation

As we move through the lesson study, we wanted to provide you with a resource to support you in planning for implementation in your classroom. Many of you requested a model of what this planning could look like. This is one perspective of approaching a Connecting Evidence to Claims lesson sequence over the 3-week span. Feel free to refer back to this planning calendar to support your own planning and implementation.

If you have any trouble at all joining the lesson study, please don’t hesitate to reach out to your NWP Thinking Partner. And, of course, please reach out with any other questions you have about participating in the lesson study.

Connecting Evidence to Claims

Try this resource out with your students to teach them to use evidence from source material in order to support their claim. The resource layers annotated reading, reflective writing, and critical thinking to support students as they gather information from texts, consider multiple angles on a topic, curate and quote source material, and finish a draft.
