Course Overview

Welcome to the SMLC Using Source Material Purposefully course, the second in a yearlong series of courses designed to support elementary teachers in the Missouri Show Me Literacies Collaborative (SMLC). For details about the entire 2023-24 course series, click here.

During this course in the elementary strand, we will explore ways to support student writers as they advance their arguments with evidence and use source material more purposefully.

You’ll notice this new course is organized by topic rather than time-bound weeks. Expect to engage in approximately six hours of coursework across the two weeks. You have access to the entire course, so feel free to work at a pace that works best for you and your schedule! This course officially ends on February 18 and the second SMLC lesson study is scheduled to begin on March 4th.

Certificate Criteria

This certificate is for six learning hours, to be completed by February 18th, 2024. To earn the credential for this course participants will:

  • Try out the Harris Moves and reflect on the process

  • Analyze and annotate student writing for forwarding and countering moves

  • Post to all discussions, respond to others, and submit a reflection about your experience in the discussions

  • Write a final reflection about your course experience

💬 Welcome Back!

Before you begin the course, we invite you to reconnect with our course community. Here are some ideas for you to respond to in the discussion:

  • How is your school year going? Share a picture of your classroom or some student work.

  • What have you tried from the first course? Share a success story or some student writing.

  • What are you looking forward to in this new course?

Be sure to respond to the posts of others!
