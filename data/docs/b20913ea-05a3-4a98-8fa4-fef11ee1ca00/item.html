🎯 Purpose

This page helps you support Missouri educators participating in their first year of the Literacy Th@t Works professional learning experience.

Your role is to guide learning, encourage reflection, and support classroom application throughout the online courses.

👥 More Than Just a Facilitator

Think of yourself as a learning partner, not a course cop. You help set the tone, build trust, and keep momentum going. Teachers are juggling a lot. You make this experience smoother, more human, and more connected.

Here's what you're really doing:

  • Encouraging participation and sharing.

  • Connecting ideas across modules.

  • Prompting reflection that leads to real change in classrooms.

  • Celebrating progress—big and small.

🔍 Focus Areas

Each module is self-paced, flexible, and rooted in practice. Your job is to help teachers:

  • Understand and try out routine literacy practices.

  • Reflect on what they're doing and why.

  • Apply strategies with students and notice what happens.

  • Earn recognition that matters to them.

Every teacher brings a different context. Help them see patterns, adapt ideas, and share insights with one another.

1️⃣ Set Up Your Profile

Help your colleagues find and connect with you by completing your profile.

Here's what to include:

  • Name — How you'd like to appear in the community

  • Tagline — A quick snapshot of your role and specialty

  • Bio — Tell us about your work in 2-3 sentences. What's your role? Who do you support? What drives your work?

  • Photo — Add a professional headshot so people can recognize you

2️⃣ Participate in a Discussion

Ready to meet your community? This is your chance to start building relationships and get a feel for how we support each other here.

Here's how to make your first connections:

  • Introduce Yourself — Open the discussion sidebar and share a bit about who you are.

  • Hopes, Dreams, Fears — What do you hope to learn or share this year?

Click on the discussion icon above to open the discussion sidebar.

3️⃣ Create an About Me Page

People connect through stories.

Create an About Me page that helps others understand who you are beyond your job title.

Start with these prompts:

  • What brought you to this work?

  • What strengths and resources do you bring to the table?

  • What's one thing outside of work that brings you joy?

Feel free to add photos or other media that help tell your story. When you're happy with it, publish the page to your profile so others can get to know the real you.
