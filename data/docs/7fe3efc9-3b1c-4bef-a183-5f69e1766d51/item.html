Preparing to Teach

In this module, you’ll step into the role of teacher-researcher by focusing on one strategy from Modules 1–3. You may have already tried several of these routines in your classroom, but for this inquiry cycle, choose one that you especially want to pay closer attention to. The goal is to look carefully at how your students engage with it and what you can learn from their responses.

Remember: this module includes three connected discussions—one before teaching, one during teaching, and one after teaching. You’ll participate in all three to complete the cycle and earn the discussion badge.
