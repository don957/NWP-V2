🧭 Further Exploration: Choosing Images

How can you ensure your classroom culture is a safe place where diversity is respected and valued? When choosing texts of any kind—written, visual, audio, video—are you honoring students in your classroom as well as the diversity of the larger society? We can be purposeful when selecting texts by paying attention to authors, topics, and representation.
